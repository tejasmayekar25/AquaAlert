AquaAlert Architecture

Autonomous IoT Waterlogging Detection & Early Warning System

AquaAlert is an end-to-end flood monitoring solution designed to detect rising water levels, trigger instant local alarms, and visualize risk patterns in real time through an integrated telemetry console and geospatial analytics engine.

📌 Project Purpose

🎯

The Problem

Urban waterlogging and sudden drainage overflows frequently paralyze transit corridors before municipal authorities can react. Manual inspections during severe downpours are hazardous and slow.

💡

The Solution

AquaAlert uses ruggedized ultrasonic sensors to continuously measure water air gaps, derives percentage-based overflow risk, and triggers local buzzer alarms while streaming data to Flask and SQLite.

⚡

Key Capabilities

  • • Sub-second ultrasonic distance sampling
  • • Canonical server-side risk state calculation
  • • WebSocket broadcasting via Flask-SocketIO
  • • SQLite historical logging & crash resilience
  • • India geospatial flood dataset forecasting

⚙️ Data Flow & Lifecycle

1

Sense

The AJ-SR04M waterproof ultrasonic transducer emits a 40 kHz pulse to measure the distance to the water surface.

2

Process & Display

The ESP32 calculates depth and risk percentage, updating the local 16x2 I2C LCD and triggering GPIO LEDs and buzzer alert patterns.

3

Ingest & Validate

The ESP32 pushes JSON telemetry via HTTP POST to /api/data. The Flask backend validates bounds and recalculates the single source of truth status.

4

Persist & Broadcast

Readings are recorded to SQLite in WAL mode and broadcast over WebSockets to all connected dashboard clients instantaneously.
